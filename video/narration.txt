Finding a cofounder should not depend on luck, one event,
or who already happens to be in your network.

FoundPair turns skills, values, working style, and practical
constraints into transparent, explainable recommendations.

A founder describes what they bring, what they need, and how
they want to build. Hard dealbreakers are checked before ranking.

The matching model ranks eligible profiles for skill coverage,
values, goals, working style, and logistics.

Every match explains what could click, surfaces one real point
of friction, and suggests questions to test the relationship.

When a match feels promising, FoundPair creates an editable,
profile-grounded introduction and saves it on the device.

FoundPair makes the first cofounder conversation more intentional.
The MVP is live now, with verified profiles and mutual introductions next.
